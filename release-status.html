LudoPaws release status

Last documented: 27 October 2026. The 1.0.0-rc9 Family V2 candidate was in Google Play production review at the last Console verification. Version 1.0.0-rc10-referral is available in internal testing to eligible testers. This dated status is not a statement that the app has since been approved or publicly launched.

Before installing

Check whether the official Google Play listing is currently available in your region. An unavailable listing means you should not assume the game is released.

Current candidate capabilities

Multiplayer and fixed quick-chat reactions are supported by the Family candidate. Free-text chat and voice are disabled. Google Play purchase and ad configurations are dependent on store/provider approval and production acceptance tests.

Optional friendship rewards

Referral rewards remain OFF. Published invitation terms describe the optional inviter-only bundle; sharing or installing alone does not earn it. Actual invite-install verification and reward fulfillment must pass real-device acceptance before activation. Operational readiness and automated tests are not Google or worldwide legal approval.

Read Privacy, Terms, Pricing and Account deletion. Products and features depend on the deployed configuration and provider eligibility.
