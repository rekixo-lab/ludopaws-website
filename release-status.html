LudoPaws release status

Last documented: 25 October 2026. The 1.0.0-rc9 Family V2 candidate was submitted to Google Play production review. This historical status is not a statement that the app has since been approved or publicly launched.

Before installing

Check whether the official Google Play listing is currently available in your region. An unavailable listing means you should not assume the game is released.

Current candidate capabilities

Multiplayer and fixed quick-chat reactions are supported by the Family candidate. Free-text chat and voice are disabled. Google Play purchase and ad configurations are dependent on store/provider approval and production acceptance tests.
