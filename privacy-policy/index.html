YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ludo Paws, published under the Rekixo brand, processes information when you play the Android game and use its online services.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Publisher contact: AI@rekixo.com. Ludo Paws is a mobile Ludo-style game. The information processed depends on the features you use and which optional services are enabled in the version you play.

1. Information the game processes

  • Account and profile: an internal account identifier, display name, account status, and links to Google identity, Friend ID or leaderboard identity when you choose those features. Ludo Paws does not ask for your Google password.
  • Authentication and security: session identifiers and expiry/revocation state, token hashes, sign-in nonces, request identifiers and limited anti-abuse/rate-limit records.
  • Gameplay and fairness: online match identifiers, pseudonymous player identifiers, seats and colours, game state, moves, results, timestamps, ruleset identifiers, dice commitments, proof digests and event history. Offline game state and progression may remain on your device.
  • Friends and social features: Friend IDs, display names, requests, relationships, invitations, recent-player entries and conversation content when you use those features.
  • Optional voice chat: if you start voice chat during a Friends match and grant microphone permission, the app captures microphone audio and sends it to participating players over WebRTC. The game API relays connection setup data; the current client does not upload the voice stream to the game API for recording or storage. Depending on the network route, other participants and connection providers may receive network connection information.
  • Store and rewards: gem balance, selected and owned cosmetics, cosmetic/reward progress when enabled, Google Play product identifiers and hashed purchase references used to verify purchases and prevent duplicate grants. Google Play handles payment details; Ludo Paws does not request full payment-card details.
  • Device-local data: settings, local profile/progression statistics, offline state and cached account information.
  • Technical connection data: hosting and network providers may process IP address, request metadata and device/network information to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. The API derives a one-way IP hash for rate limiting and does not intentionally store raw IP addresses in match or rate-limit state; providers may maintain their own operational logs.
  • Advertising data, when ads are enabled: Google Mobile Ads/AdMob may process ad request information, advertising/device identifiers, consent signals and ad interactions under Google's terms and privacy policy. Rewarded ads are opt-in. Google consent tools may offer consent or privacy options where required.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to create and restore guest or linked accounts; authenticate sessions; provide offline and online games; operate friends, conversations, invitations and leaderboards; sync server-backed wallet and cosmetic entitlements; verify purchases and rewarded-ad callbacks; protect game integrity; prevent fraud, replay and duplicate rewards; troubleshoot the service; and respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

Depending on the features enabled, providers include Cloudflare for hosting, API and server-side storage; the configured EntroNex service for the online dice-proof workflow; Google identity services for sign-in; Google Play Games Services for supported game/social integrations; Google Play for app distribution and in-app purchases/subscriptions when available; Google's public STUN service for optional voice-chat connection setup; and Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Google's consent tools when advertising is enabled. Providers process information as needed to provide their services, protect systems, verify transactions/rewards or comply with law. Their own privacy notices govern their independent processing.

Ludo Paws does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect players, prevent abuse, address security incidents or enforce legitimate claims. Do not send passwords, access/refresh tokens, purchase tokens or guest deletion codes to support.

4. Purchases and ads

Google Play displays the available product, localized price and currency before purchase. The server verifies purchases before applying eligible Gems or entitlements. Product availability, checkout and subscriptions depend on the live Google Play and server configuration; this policy does not promise that every feature is currently enabled. Where rewarded ads are enabled, the server verifies the ad reward callback before crediting the configured reward. Ads and consent behavior must be checked against the actual release configuration.

5. Storage and security

The Android app uses Android Keystore-backed protection for account credentials and selected sensitive local records where implemented. App API traffic uses HTTPS, and server verification—not a client-only callback or local wallet cache—controls paid entitlements. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account secure and do not share credentials or deletion codes.

6. Retention

Account, profile, wallet, cosmetics, social, leaderboard and session records are retained while the account/features are active and are removed through the account-deletion process, subject to the limited records described below. Sessions expire or may be revoked.

Online match-proof receipts: retained for up to 365 days after a match finishes, or 365 days after creation if the match does not finish. These records contain match/ruleset identifiers, pseudonymous player identifiers, game events and dice-proof commitments/digests, and are designed not to contain display names or session credentials. Expired receipts are scheduled for deletion and checked for expiry when accessed.

Limited security records: minimal deletion tombstones and hashed guest request/deletion credentials may remain as long as needed to prevent deleted accounts or credentials from being recreated or replayed. The current implementation does not set a fixed expiry for every such marker. Google Play keeps its own order history; other providers may retain operational logs under their own policies. Local offline data or cache may remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall.

7. Account and data deletion

In the app: open Profile → menu → Delete account and data and confirm. The server deletion flow removes the account profile, active sessions, wallet and Gems, cosmetic inventory and rewarded-ad progress, account-linked app purchase references, linked Google identity, Friend/social records and leaderboard profile. The app also attempts to clear the matching local profile, wallet, pending-purchase, friend and leaderboard caches.

On the web: visit Account and Data Deletion. Google-linked accounts require verification with the Google identity previously linked to Ludo Paws and explicit confirmation. An unlinked guest account can use the optional delete-only code created in the app before uninstalling. The code can delete an eligible guest account only; it cannot sign in or recover progress. If server deletion succeeds but local cleanup fails, clear Ludo Paws storage on that device.

Deletion does not delete your Google account, Google's independent Play order/payment history, or separate data controlled by independent providers. The receipt and security-marker retention described above may continue after account deletion. If you cannot complete the process, contact AI@rekixo.com. Ownership verification may be required. Do not email passwords, tokens, full payment details or deletion codes.

8. Children and age-related settings

The publisher's target-audience and age-rating declarations, child-directed treatment and consent requirements must match the final Google Play listing and the features/providers enabled in the release. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information processed through Ludo Paws, contact the publisher using the address below.

9. Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to link a Google identity, use optional online/social features and start optional voice chat. You may request account deletion as described above. For privacy questions or a request you cannot complete through the available flow, contact AI@rekixo.com.
