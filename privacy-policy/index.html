YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This page explains the privacy topics LudoPaws users should be able to understand before playing.

Owner review required: This draft intentionally avoids asserting unverified data practices. Before public use, the LudoPaws operator must complete and verify the details below against the production app, backend, third-party SDKs, and applicable law.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use, an online game may process information such as account or guest identifiers, gameplay and session data, device and app diagnostics, and information you submit to support. The operator must specify which categories LudoPaws actually collects and which are optional.

2. How information is used

Information should be described according to its actual purpose, such as operating gameplay, maintaining service security, troubleshooting errors, preventing abuse, and responding to support requests. The operator must remove any purpose that does not apply and disclose any additional purpose that does.

3. Storage, service providers, and sharing

The final policy must identify relevant hosting providers, analytics or crash-reporting tools, advertising or payment partners if used, and the circumstances in which information is shared. It should also explain applicable retention periods and safeguards.

4. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the type of data and operational or legal requirements. For guidance on guest data and requesting help with account or data deletion, see Guest Account & Data. The operator must insert the verified request channel, process, and expected timelines before publication.

5. Children and age requirements

The operator must state the intended age group, any age restrictions, and the process followed if information from a child is identified, consistent with applicable law and actual product design.

6. Security and international processing

The operator should describe appropriate security measures and disclose relevant international transfers or hosting locations where required. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your information. The final policy must provide a working contact method and explain how to submit a request.

8. Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, the operator should update this page and provide any notice required by law or the app's design.

9. Contact

Before publication, add the verified privacy contact email or support URL operated by the LudoPaws team. Do not publish an unmonitored or unverified contact address.

Effective date: To be set by the service owner after review.
