ACCOUNT HELP

Guest Account & Data

How guest sessions, progress recovery and deletion work in Ludo Paws.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What is a guest account?

The game can create a server-backed guest account without a full registration flow. The app keeps session and recovery credentials protected using Android Keystore-backed storage where implemented. You can choose to link the account to a Google identity using the in-app account feature.

Will my progress transfer to another device?

Do not assume an unlinked guest account or device-local profile statistics will transfer after reinstalling or changing devices. Local match history and progression statistics remain device-local and are not automatically uploaded. A guest account that was never linked to Google may depend on the original installation's recovery information; save any recovery guidance the app provides before uninstalling.

How do I delete my account?

While you can open the app: go to Profile → menu → Delete account and data and confirm the permanent deletion request.

After uninstalling: an unlinked guest account can be deleted using the guest deletion code created in the app before uninstalling. Use the official Account and Data Deletion page and enter the code only there. The code is for deletion only; it cannot sign in or recover your account. If the guest account was linked to Google, use the Google verification route on that page instead.

What happens when deletion completes?

The server deletion flow removes the account profile, active sessions, wallet and Gems, cosmetic inventory and rewarded-ad progress, account-linked app purchase references, linked Google identity, Friend/social records and leaderboard profile. The app attempts to clear corresponding local caches. If local cleanup fails, clear the app's storage on that device. Online match-proof receipts may be retained for up to 365 days after match completion (or creation for an unfinished match); minimal hashed or pseudonymous security markers may remain to prevent replay or account recreation. Google Play's independent order history and provider records are not deleted by Ludo Paws.

If I cannot access the app or deletion page

Contact AI@rekixo.com and explain that you need help with an account or data request. The publisher may need to verify ownership. Never email passwords, session/access tokens, purchase tokens, full payment details or a guest deletion code.
