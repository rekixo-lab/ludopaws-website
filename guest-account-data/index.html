ACCOUNT HELP

Guest Account
& Data

Information for players who use LudoPaws without creating or linking a full account.

Important: The exact guest-account behavior depends on the app's implementation. The service owner must verify the instructions below against the released Android app and backend before publication.

What is a guest account?

Guest access lets a player use the features made available by the game without completing a full account-registration flow. The app should explain whether guest progress is stored on the device, on the service, or both, and whether it can be recovered after reinstalling or changing devices.

Will I lose my progress?

Do not assume guest progress will transfer between devices or survive uninstalling the app unless the game explicitly confirms that it is backed up and recoverable. Before clearing app data, uninstalling, or changing devices, review the current in-app account guidance.

How do I request data access or deletion?

Use the support or account-management method provided inside the current LudoPaws app. The service owner should replace this paragraph with the verified steps, identify what data can be deleted, explain any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons, and state the expected response time.

What if I cannot access the app?

The service owner must provide a monitored support email or web form here for requests that cannot be made from inside the app. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other secret credentials when requesting help.

What should I include in a request?

Provide only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant guest session or account, using the app's documented process. Never send passwords or one-time authentication codes. The operator should explain any verification needed to protect account ownership.
