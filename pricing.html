Where to see the price

Google Play supplies the localized price and currency shown in the app and purchase confirmation. Product availability, price and applicable tax depend on Google Play and the country or region for your account. Check the final confirmation before paying. This website does not take payments or offer a separate web checkout.

Paws Plus monthly subscription

paws_plus is the source-code subscription ID. Where an eligible offer is live, Google Play shows its current price, billing period and terms before purchase. The intended base plan ID is monthly-auto; this ID alone does not prove the offer is active. Paws Plus suppresses eligible forced post-win interstitial ads while the server-verified subscription is active. Optional rewarded ads remain a player choice.

The subscription is intended to renew monthly until canceled through Google Play. Cancellation stops future renewal; access may continue through the paid period and ends when the server verifies expiry or revocation. Grace periods, account holds, refunds and reversals follow the Google Play subscription state and applicable consumer rules. Do not rely on a cached in-app status as proof of an active entitlement.

Verification, virtual currency and refunds

Gems are virtual in-game currency, not a bank balance, cash equivalent or cash-redemption service. The server verifies a Google Play purchase before granting Gems or an entitlement. Pending, invalid, mismatched, expired, revoked or unverified transactions must not create an active entitlement; retries should not grant a duplicate reward.

Google Play handles payment collection and retains its own order/payment history. Use Google Play order history and its official support/refund process for an order or refund request, subject to its rules and applicable consumer rights. If a confirmed order has not appeared in the game, contact AI@rekixo.com. Do not send passwords, access/refresh tokens, purchase tokens, full payment details or guest deletion codes.
