ACCOUNT CONTROL

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Ludo Paws account and associated server-managed account data.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Ludo Paws and go to Profile → menu → Delete account and data.
  2. Review the permanent-deletion warning and confirm.
  3. Wait for the app to confirm the server-side deletion result. If local cleanup fails, clear Ludo Paws app storage on that device.

Option 2: Request help from publisher support

If you cannot access the app or complete the deletion flow, email AI@rekixo.com with the subject “Ludo Paws account deletion request”. Explain that you want your Ludo Paws account deleted and include only the minimum information needed to help identify the account. The publisher may ask you to verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, access/refresh tokens, purchase tokens, full payment details or guest deletion codes.

Unlinked guest accounts

If you still have the app, use its guest deletion-code feature before uninstalling and keep the code private. An unlinked guest account may be deleted with the delete-only code on the dedicated deletion flow when that flow is configured. The code cannot sign in or recover an account. If you already uninstalled without saving the code, contact publisher support for an ownership-verification process.

What deletion removes

The server deletion flow is designed to remove the account profile, active sessions, wallet and Gems, cosmetic inventory and rewarded-ad progress, account-linked app purchase references, linked Google identity, Friend/social records and leaderboard profile. The app attempts to clear related local caches.

What may remain

Online match-proof receipts may be retained for up to 365 days after match completion, or 365 days after creation for an unfinished match. Minimal hashed or pseudonymous security markers may remain as needed to prevent replay or account recreation. Google Play's separate order/payment history, independent provider records and data already stored offline on your device are not deleted by Ludo Paws.

Read the Privacy Policy for retention and provider details, and the Guest Account & Data page for guest recovery limitations.

This page provides instructions and a support request route. The separate self-service web deletion flow requires its Google OAuth client configuration and production end-to-end verification before it can be represented as an active self-service form.
